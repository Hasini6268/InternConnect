About InternConnect

InternConnect is a complete Internship Management System designed to connect students, companies, and administrators through one easy-to-use platform. Students can explore internships and apply online, companies can manage hiring, and administrators can monitor the entire recruitment process efficiently.

Platform Features

🎓 Students

Browse internships, submit applications, track application status and manage your career.

🏢 Companies

Post internships, manage applicants, shortlist candidates and hire efficiently.

👨‍💼 Administrator

Monitor users, internships, applications and overall platform activities.
